Service 02

Diesel pumps & injectors

Complete parts and service for mechanical and electronic diesel injection systems. Our state-of-the-art test benches — made from high-precision equipment — together with our team of qualified technicians guarantee high quality when repairing common rail, injection pumps and diesel injectors.

Pump types serviced: Cummins celect and non-celect, Rotary, DPA, CAV rotary and inline, VE, VA, Bosch inline and VE, Zexel and Denso pumps and injectors.

Parts stocked: pump heads, rotors, elements, seal kits, governors, cam discs, springs, barrels, plungers, nozzles and more.

Service 03

Engine & cylinder head reconditioning

Complete engine reconditioning and sub-assemblies are done to OEM specifications. Our personnel have over 20 years of hands-on experience in engine rebuilding.

Cylinder head reconditioning uses modern state-of-the-art skimming machines, valve refacers, pressure testing and valve seat cutting machines. Digital measurement systems ensure accurate, highly precise workmanship.

Extensive experience reconditioning: Deutz, Detroit, Perkins, ADE, Cummins and all passenger engines.

Service 04

Auto electrics Harare

Lysam is a supplier of auto electrical products sourced from top international brands including Bosch, Hella, VDO, Delco Remy, Transpozen, Unipoint, Ring, Elpa and Control Instrument.

  • Starter motors — new starters, solenoids, armatures and repair kits
  • Alternators — new alternators, regulators, rectifiers and stators
  • Accessories — relay units, flashers, LED lamps, spot lights, beacon lights, oil pressure and temperature gauges

All repairs and services done by qualified auto electrical staff and tested by state-of-the-art test benches. Products cover light motor vehicles, heavy duty vehicles and earth moving equipment.

Service 05

Hydraulic cylinder reconditioning

Complete reconditioning of hydraulic cylinders done in partnership with competent partners using dimensional reclamation equipment to save on costs.

Stock of hydraulic seals and induction hardened shafts are kept for fast turnaround. Every hydraulic cylinder is thoroughly tested on our test bench for bypass and leaks at operating pressures as a quality control measure.
