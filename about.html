Our story

Engineering trust,
one repair at a time.

Lysam Turbo and Diesel is a wholly owned Zimbabwean company founded on Reliability, Responsiveness, Empathy and Communication — bringing global standards to Harare and Zimbabwe.

Our mission

Global standards.
Local expertise.

Lysam Turbo and Diesel brings global engineering standards to the areas of turbocharger repairs, diesel fuel injection systems, cylinder head reconditioning, just-in-time parts supplies, vehicle servicing and hydraulic cylinder reconditioning.

The key objective is to offer value to all our customers and stakeholders — not just through technical excellence but through reliability, honest communication and genuine care for your equipment downtime.

We are a one-stop-shop in engine reconditioning, carrying most processes internally and ensuring high levels of control for the final product — and full workmanship warranty.
